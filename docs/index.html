The tour

Built for people who keep a lot running.

Coding agents, simulators, design tools, forty browser tabs. Vitals shows what each one costs and lets you do something about it.

01 · LIVE

One chart. Three numbers. Every core.

CPU, GPU and memory share one live chart, so you see them move together. Hover anywhere on it and the big numbers rewind to that moment.

02 · APPS

Who's using your Mac, heaviest first.

One list ordered by real load: CPU, memory and GPU together. Values turn orange, then red, as they get heavy.

  • Quit anything from its row, with a force quit if it hangs
  • The list holds still under your pointer, so nothing jumps
  • Helpers are counted toward the app that owns them
03 · AI AGENTS

See exactly what your agents are doing.

Every Claude Code session with the commands it is running right now. Codex, Gemini CLI, Aider and other agents show up the moment they start. Stop a task or a whole session from its card.

04 · BOOST

Free memory in one click.

Where your memory went, as one bar. Tick the apps you don't need, press Boost, and see what you got back. Idle iOS Simulators shut themselves down once your agents go quiet.

05 · BATTERY CARE

A battery that lasts years longer.

Batteries wear fastest when they sit full and hot. Vitals keeps yours below full and tells you when heat is the problem, and which app causes it.

  • Charge limit at 80, 85, 90 or 95%, or off
  • Charge to 100% once, for a long day away from power
  • Health, cycles and a notification when it runs hot
06 · HISTORY

From the last 15 minutes to the last month.

Click any number for its history, with who was responsible at each point. Kept on your Mac, about 5 MB a year.

Watch it work

Your agent types. Vitals sees it.

Every command an agent runs shows up in Vitals the moment it starts, with what it costs, and leaves when it's done. Stop anything with one click.
